Description

NO ONWARD CHAIN- GREAT VALUE!!!

A fantastic opportunity to acquire this 3 Bedroom terraced home in a popular area of Taunton. Offered with no onward chain and presenting great potential as a home or rental investment.

The ground floor provides spacious living accommodation with a lounge and kitchen/diner upstairs are three good sized bedrooms and family bathroom. Outside the benefits include enclosed gardens to the front and rear of the property.

Laurel Close is perfectly positioned for convenience, situated less than a mile from Taunton Railway Station for easy commuting to Bristol and London. Local schools and the amenities of Kingston Road are within easy walking distance.
