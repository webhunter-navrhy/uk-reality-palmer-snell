Description

* NO ONWARD CHAIN*

Located within Yeovil Town Centre and therefore within close proximity of local shops and amenities, this beautifully presented three bedroom terraced home is full of character and ready to move straight into. Arranged over three floors, the property offers generous, light filled accommodation with high ceilings and charming original features throughout.

The ground floor includes a spacious living/dining room ideal for everyday living and entertaining, a well appointed kitchen, and a versatile sunroom overlooking the garden, perfect as a cosy sitting area or a home workspace. The first floor provides two well proportioned double bedrooms and a stylish main bathroom, while the top floor is dedicated to an impressive master bedroom offering a private retreat.

Outside, the rear garden provides a lovely space for relaxing or dining outdoors, and the property also benefits from its OWN PARKING SPACE.

Combining character, space, and practicality, this move in ready home is an excellent choice for a wide range of buyers. Early viewing is highly recommended to appreciate all it offers.
